# GICS-NGFS-sectoral-mapping
A mapping to link NGFS and GICS sectoral classifications, based on sectors' GHG emissions materiality.

Portfolio climate-alignment exercises are limited by the mismatch between the sectoral classifications used by asset managers and those of climate scenarios. To address this issue, this article focuses on a NGFS–GICS mapping, with NACE as the pivot classification. It is based on the materiality of Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions rather than on semantic similarity. Then, this mapping is incorporated into a Sectoral Decarbonization Approach that down-scales NGFS climate trajectories into GICS sector pathways. This approach adjusts the GICS sector pathways at a portfolio level with adjustment for regional exposure. Finally, this approach is applied on two illustrative ETF indices. The results show how differences in sectoral composition and regional trajectories affect a portfolio’s financed GHG emissions trajectory. The proposed framework is transparent, asset-class agnostic and can be used as such by financial professionals for internal works such as climate stress-tests. The current GICS-NGFS mapping can be completed and expanded to better fit one’s needs.
